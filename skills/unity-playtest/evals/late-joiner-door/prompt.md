A friend joined our session late, after I had already unlocked the door, and said the level looked wrong on their screen. Host a session, take the key, open the door and pick up a coin, then have a second player join and tell me what the late joiner sees compared with the host. The project in this folder (Unity-Playtest-Sample) is open in the Unity Editor, and Unity's Pipeline CLI (`unity`) is on PATH. You may make a temporary development build for the client, with the Pipeline runtime if you need it, but leave the project the way you found it.
